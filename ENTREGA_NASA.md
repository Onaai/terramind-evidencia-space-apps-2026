# TerraMind Evidencia — material para la entrega

## Título

TerraMind Evidence: Can We Trust the Trend?

## Resumen en inglés

TerraMind turns an environmental question into a traceable evidence record. Our pilot examines vegetation and rainfall at a fixed location in Argentina's Gran Chaco using 253 real MODIS vegetation composites and 4,018 daily GPM IMERG precipitation estimates from 2015–2025. Users can inspect observations, quality exclusions, seasonal anomalies, Sen slopes and exploratory Mann–Kendall results, then compare predefined analysis windows. The app exposes uncertainty and withholds significance claims when its conditions are not met. It includes an accessible Spanish interface, a scientific review view and downloadable evidence. This is a single-site pilot at two different spatial resolutions, not a regional diagnosis or an operational warning system.

## Problema y propuesta

Public environmental data are available, but understanding what supports a claim requires tracing quality filters, missing observations, time windows and statistical assumptions. TerraMind makes those decisions visible in a local, reproducible workflow. We build on NASA products and existing analysis methods rather than claiming a new trend algorithm.

## Qué funciona

Interactive satellite context map; real NDVI, EVI and precipitation series; time-period filters; explicit vegetation QA; seasonal reference; annual aggregation; Sen and conditional exploratory Mann–Kendall; predefined sensitivity comparisons; source receipts and hashes; CSV, SVG and Markdown export. A separate project view presents future hypotheses without invented commercial metrics.

## Resultado real y limitaciones

For 2015–2025, the strict NDVI pilot yields Sen −0.01152289 index/year and exploratory p=0.27575787; EVI yields +0.00174139 and p=0.64042875. IMERG annual rainfall yields −0.95285714 mm/year and p=1. These results do not support a significant monotonic trend at α=0.05 under the implemented checks; they do not prove stability or identify causes. MODIS and IMERG have different grid sizes. One MODIS collection match remains unresolved; the POWER response does not disclose IMERG collection/run or retrieval QA. No claims of deforestation, ecological recovery or fire prediction are made.
